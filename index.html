Copyright Disclaimer

All screensavers uploaded to this community repository are provided by individual users. If you believe your copyrighted work has been uploaded without authorization, please follow our DMCA Takedown process below.

DMCA Takedown Notice Procedure

To file a copyright infringement notice, please provide the following details:

  • Direct link to the infringing image in our catalog.
  • Proof of ownership or authorization from the copyright holder.
  • Your contact information (email address).

Send notice via GitHub Issue labeled dmca-takedown or email the maintainers at takedown@readerbackdrop-catalog.org. Takedown requests are processed within 48 hours.
